About

We are a team of students competing in the Victorian state finals of STEM Racing - an international engineering competition.

Who are we?

We are Nexus. A STEM Racing team comprised of five students from Trinity Grammar School, Kew. We have a passion for innovation, engineering, and teamwork.

  • United by our shared love for STEM, we aim to push the bounds of innovation, creating designs that excel on the track.
  • With 3 podium finishes under our belt, we are back once again, ready to showcase our skills and dedication, as we reach for new heights.
  • We are competing in the 2026 Victorian State Finals, in late October. We're ready to race, ready to perform, and ready to prove ourselves once more.
  • Follow us on Instagram for updates on our journey.

What is STEM Racing?

Proudly supported by Formula 1, STEM Racing is an exciting competition, engaging students in the design, construction and racing of miniature F1 Cars.

  • STEM Racing acquaints students with many real-life skills, from engineering to project management, and marketing.
  • It gives students hands-on experience with design software, manufacturing tools, and presentation skills, all in a team-based environment.
  • Participants work to strict industry standards, creating professional portfolios, marketing strategies, and engineering innovations to compete at the highest level, striving to be crowned World Champions.

Our Track Record

With national podium finishes and a drive to improve each season, our journey reflects determination, resilience, and a commitment to excellence.

SF24

Team Aerosonic, a Y7 Cadet Class team that would later become part of Nexus. Nexus was also launched with the goal of unity through collaboration and shared success.

NF25

After success at States, Nexus, (now unified with Aerosonic), achieved 2nd place in racing and 3rd place overall.

SF25 & NF26

At states Nexus proved themselves with a 2nd place finish, and at Nationals placed 7th with 3 major awards.

SF26

After a successful National Finals, Nexus finds themselves back at the start of a brand new season, with a reimagined logo, refined racecar and legendary portfolios.
